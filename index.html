Design & Branding Implementation Statement

This project adopts the official design identity of Trimex Connect (College of Computer Studies, Trimex Colleges). The design strictly avoids generic AI aesthetics—such as synthetic neon blur blobs or artificial floating emojis—in favor of a purposeful institutional palette (Trimex deep maroon, cardinal red, charcoal slate, and crisp white), typography powered by Google Font Poppins, the official Trimex Connect logo, and the authentic portal UI walkthrough animation.
